What is Matra Boli?

A free caption studio for Indian creators. Drop in a short video and Matra Boli listens to it, writes captions in Hindi, Hinglish or 12 more Indian languages, and animates them Reels-style — ready to download. No sign-up, no API keys, and your video never leaves your device.

Popular Languages

Built for Indian creators

14 Indian languages

Hindi, Hinglish, Punjabi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Odia, Assamese, Urdu and English.

12 caption animations

Word Pop, Karaoke, Typewriter, Bounce, Glitch, Wave and more. Mix any animation with any style.

Native-script fonts

Each script gets a matching font from the Baloo family, so Devanagari, Tamil, Bengali and the rest render properly.

Your video stays private

Speech recognition and translation run on your own device. No account, no uploads, no API keys.
