Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

The short version: your video never leaves your device. Matra Boli runs its AI inside your browser. We don't have accounts, we don't collect your videos or captions, and we don't use analytics or advertising trackers.

1. Who we are

Matra Boli is a free tool made by Nishkam under the Nishtek name ("we", "us"). You can reach us on Instagram at @nishkambhatia.

2. What happens to your video

  • When you choose a video, your browser reads it locally. Its audio is decoded and transcribed on your device. Translation and caption rendering also happen on your device.
  • Your video, audio, transcript and captions are not uploaded to us or to any third party.
  • Exported files are created on your device and saved by your browser.

3. What is stored in your browser

Matra Boli uses your browser's own storage for:

  • Model cache: the downloaded AI model files, so you don't download them again.
  • Drafts and presets: only when you click "Save Draft" or save a preset. A draft holds the caption text and settings, not the video.
  • Small preferences, such as whether you've hidden the intro banner.

All of this stays on your device. You can delete it any time by clearing site data for Matra Boli in your browser settings.

4. Third-party services your browser connects to

To load the app, your browser downloads files from these providers. Like any website request, they can see your IP address and basic browser information, but they never receive your video or captions:

  • GitHub Pages (nishtek.in): hosts the Matra Boli website itself and keeps standard web-server logs, such as IP address and pages requested.
  • Hugging Face (huggingface.co): AI model files for speech recognition and translation.
  • jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net): the Transformers.js library that runs the models.
  • unpkg (unpkg.com): the icon library.
  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com): fonts for the interface and for captions in Indian scripts.

Each provider has its own privacy policy. Links to Instagram take you to Instagram, which has its own policy too.

5. What we don't do

  • We don't require an account or ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.
  • We don't sell or share personal data, because we don't collect any.

6. Children

Matra Boli is a general-purpose creator tool and doesn't knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

7. Changes

If we change how Matra Boli handles data, for example by adding an online feature, we'll update this page and the date above before the change goes live.

8. Contact

Questions? Message @nishkambhatia on Instagram.
